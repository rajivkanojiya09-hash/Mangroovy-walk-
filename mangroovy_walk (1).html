WHY MANGROVES MATTER — IN 1 MIN

Mangroves are coastal ecosystems made up of salt-tolerant plants and trees. Around Mumbai, they form an important natural buffer between land and the sea.

Mangroves help reduce the impact of coastal flooding, store carbon, provide habitat for birds and wildlife, trap sediments, and support healthy coastal ecosystems.

THREATS

Learn what students can safely observe in the field.

Plastic

Why harmful: Plastic can persist in the environment and may affect wildlife and waterways.

Observe: visible bags, bottles, packaging and fragments.

Do: record the location and inform an adult or appropriate authority. Do not touch unknown waste.

Debris Dumping

Why harmful: dumped material can obstruct water flow and damage habitat.

Observe: piles of construction or household debris.

Do: document from a safe public position and report through responsible adults or authorities.

Sewage / Water Pollution

Why harmful: polluted water can reduce water quality and affect aquatic life.

Observe: unusual discharge, persistent surface contamination or strong visible pollution indicators.

Do: stay away from contaminated water and report the observation.

Cutting / Habitat Damage

Why harmful: removal of vegetation can reduce habitat and coastal protection functions.

Observe: visible cutting, clearing or damaged vegetation.

Do: do not confront anyone. Record safely and tell an adult or appropriate authority.

Citizen Do's

  • Observe from safe public areas.
  • Record date, time and location.
  • Inform a teacher, adult or appropriate authority.
  • Keep evidence factual and respectful.

Citizen Don'ts

  • Never go alone.
  • Do not enter water, mud or mangrove roots.
  • Do not touch unknown waste.
  • Do not confront people dumping waste or cutting vegetation.

Mangrove Protection Pledge

I will observe mangrove areas responsibly, avoid disturbing wildlife and vegetation, record field information honestly, and share concerns through safe and appropriate channels.

Emergency / Official Reporting

Helpline: 1926

For immediate danger, follow your school's safety procedure and contact an adult or appropriate emergency service. For environmental complaints, use official municipal or government reporting channels applicable to the location.

Godrej Enterprises Group / Godrej & Boyce

Conserves the Godrej mangroves in Vikhroli and documents mangrove restoration, conservation, research and awareness work. Its Vikhroli sustainability work also includes wastewater treatment/recycling and waste-management initiatives.

Map point: Godrej / Vikhroli mangrove conservation area.

Official Website Mangroves Website

United Way Mumbai — Mission Mangroves

Works on Mumbai wetland and mangrove conservation through waste management, wetland cleaning, mangrove maintenance, restoration and community awareness. Its programme uses Safai Saathis and clean-up activities at adopted wetland sites.

Map point: Mumbai wetland / mangrove programme reference.

Official Mission Mangroves Page

Vanashakti

Mumbai environmental NGO focused on forests, mangroves, rivers and wetlands. Its documented work includes mangrove clean-up drives, awareness and protection of wetlands and mangroves.
